Hushly Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-09-25

Hushly (“we”, “our”) is a free Android application offering sleep and focus sounds, with an optional paid upgrade (Hushly Plus). This policy explains what data the app collects and how it is used.

1. Audience

Hushly is intended for users 13 years of age or older (parents and adults). It is not directed at children under 13. Although the app includes lullaby sounds parents may play for their children, the app itself is operated by an adult user.

2. Data the app collects directly

Hushly does not ask you to create an account or provide a name or email address. Payment for Hushly Plus is handled by Google Play (see below). We do not operate a server that stores your listening history. The third-party services below receive app and device data.

The following data is stored only on the user’s device via Android SharedPreferences:

  • Favorite and recently-played sound IDs
  • Onboarding completion flag
  • Sleep log (start/end times and sound IDs of Sleep and Twins sessions)
  • Saved Twins mixes
  • Whether Hushly Plus is active, the expiry time of an ad-free pass, and an app-launch counter

These local preferences are not sent by Hushly to our server and are deleted when the app is uninstalled. Playback events described below are separately sent to Firebase Analytics.

3. Third-party services

Firebase Analytics (App Usage)

Hushly uses Google Analytics for Firebase to measure app usage and investigate errors. The app sends events when it opens, when a sound starts or stops, when a timer completes, when an ad is shown (including the estimated ad revenue AdMob reports for that impression), when the Hushly Plus screen is viewed, when a purchase starts, completes, or fails, when a rewarded ad is offered or earned, and when an app error is recorded. Event details may include a sound ID and collection, playback or timer duration, ad format and revenue value, product ID and entry point, and a short error description or context. Sleep log contents are not sent. Firebase Analytics also collects basic app-usage and device information automatically, including an app-instance identifier. This information is transmitted to Google for analytics; it is not limited to the device. We do not intentionally send names or email addresses in these events. See Google’s privacy policy.

Google AdMob (Advertising)

Hushly displays ads through Google AdMob. AdMob may collect:

  • Advertising ID (resettable by the user in Android system settings)
  • Approximate (non-precise) location derived from IP
  • Device information (model, OS version, language)
  • Ad interaction data
  • Diagnostic and app interaction information

AdMob uses this information to serve and measure ads. See Google’s policy at policies.google.com/privacy.

For users in the European Economic Area (EEA), UK, and Switzerland, Hushly requests consent for personalized advertising via Google’s User Messaging Platform (UMP) before AdMob is initialized.

Hushly configures AdMob requests with:

  • tagForChildDirectedTreatment = unspecified (the app targets ages 13 and older)
  • maxAdContentRating = G by default (family-safe ad content only)

Google Play Billing (Hushly Plus)

Hushly Plus is an optional paid upgrade sold through Google Play. Payments are processed entirely by Google Play; Hushly never receives your card or other payment details. To check whether Plus is active, the app asks Google Play on your device which Hushly products your Google account owns and stores a yes/no flag locally. Purchase events (product ID, entry point, success or failure) are sent to Firebase Analytics as described above. See Google Play’s terms.

Firebase Remote Config

Hushly downloads configuration values from Firebase Remote Config (for example, how often ads may appear and quiet-hour settings). Firebase uses the app-instance identifier to deliver these values and may use it for A/B experiments.

Google Mobile Services

AdMob relies on Google Play services, which may collect diagnostic data per Google’s privacy policy linked above.

4. User rights

  • You can reset your Advertising ID or opt out of personalized ads from Android Settings → Google → Ads.
  • For EEA / UK / Swiss users, the advertising consent form is shown when Google determines it is required. The app does not currently provide a control to reopen it. This form concerns advertising; it does not control Firebase Analytics collection.
  • To remove locally stored preferences, uninstall Hushly. Uninstalling does not by itself erase analytics or advertising data already sent to Google. For questions or a data deletion request, email us at the contact address below; we will review the request and available Google data controls. This app has no account-based deletion feature.

5. Children

Hushly is not for children under 13. We do not knowingly collect data from children. If we learn we have inadvertently received data from a child under 13, we will delete it.

6. Changes

We may update this policy. The “Last updated” date at the top reflects the latest revision.
